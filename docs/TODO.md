## Sleep suggestion notification

After a short night, send a suggestion that says how much to sleep, not just how long you slept.

- **When:** last night's sleep (medium or high confidence) is clearly below the person's usual, for example at least 1 hour under their median of the last 4 weeks, or under 6 hours.
- **What it says:** how short the night was, how much sleep to aim for tonight, and a bedtime that makes that possible from the next alarm. For example: *"You slept 5 h 10 m, about 1 h 40 m less than usual. Aim for at least 7 h tonight: in bed by 23:30 for your 07:00 alarm."*
- **Timing:** once in the morning after waking (or in the evening, before the usual late-use block), at a receptive moment, and not on days the user labelled as travel or unwell.
- **Target:** the person's own usual sleep, but never below 7 h (the common adult recommendation).
- **Follow-up:** if several short nights come in a row, link it to the late-night patterns (late weekday nights, long sessions after midnight) and offer the weeknight pause.
